# Clear Ice wallpaper

`clear-ice.png` is the bundled BrowserOS 3 wallpaper, generated with the built-in
image generation tool. It is a generated image in a 3D render style, not an
interactive fluid simulation or an editable 3D scene.

Final generation prompt:

> Use case: stylized-concept
> Asset type: BrowserOS 3 desktop wallpaper, wide 16:9 landscape, high resolution.
> Primary request: A three-dimensional sculptural liquid form in glossy black with pale ice-blue tones. A fluid twisting loop with broad flowing folds, captured suspended in stillness, physically convincing smooth liquid surfaces and soft specular reflections.
> Scene/backdrop: deep near-black seamless background with a very subtle cold-blue glow.
> Composition/framing: entire sculpture visible, occupying the central-right two thirds, quiet dark negative space on the left for desktop icons; generous margins; elegant desktop background composition.
> Lighting/mood: cinematic softbox lighting, pale icy blue rim light, subdued glossy highlights, calm and refined.
> Color palette: black, charcoal, ice blue, tiny touches of silver-blue.
> Materials/textures: polished liquid obsidian, smooth continuous reflective surfaces; strong sense of depth.
> Constraints: no text, no letters, no logos, no interface, no watermark.


## Light version

`clear-ice-light.png` is the matching light version. Clear Ice switches to it
when dark mode is off. It can also be selected independently as **Clear Ice Light**
in the wallpaper picker. Both variants are bundled and use portable identifiers.

The light variant was edited with the built-in image generation tool using
`clear-ice.png` as its reference. Final edit prompt:

> Use case: lighting-weather image edit.
> Edit target: the supplied Clear Ice desktop wallpaper.
> Create the matching LIGHT THEME version of this exact wallpaper. Preserve the exact liquid sculpture shape, loops, folds, silhouette, scale, camera angle, placement and wide 16:9 framing. Keep the quiet negative space on the left for desktop icons.
> Change only the materials, lighting, and surrounding tones: replace the near-black background with a clean pearl-white to very pale ice-blue seamless background; turn the black liquid obsidian into luminous polished pearl-silver liquid with soft icy-blue reflections and gently darker blue-gray recesses. Use soft diffuse daylight, delicate blue edges, restrained highlights, and a subtle soft contact shadow on the pale ground. The result should feel airy, refined, and comfortably bright, while retaining clear sculptural depth and the identity of the original.
> No text, logos, interface, watermark, additional objects, or transparency.
